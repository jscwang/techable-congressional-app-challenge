The Problem

Loneliness is a
silent epidemic.

More than 1 million older adults in the U.K. go over a month without speaking to a friend or family member. The consequences are devastating—and underreported.

60M+
Older adults in the U.S. experience chronic loneliness and memory loss, with limited access to meaningful daily interaction.
Source: AARP, 2023
26%
Higher risk of premature mortality associated with social isolation among older adults worldwide.
Source: WHO, 2023
1:8
Average staff-to-resident ratio in senior living communities, leaving little time for meaningful daily one-on-one conversations.
Source: CQC Report, 2022
How It Works

Three steps to a
companion who truly knows them.

🗂
1
Build the memory bank

Add the resident's life story, family members, hobbies, and meaningful memories. The richer the profile, the more personal every conversation becomes.

💬
2
Start a conversation

The resident speaks naturally or types. Techable detects emotional tone, recalls relevant memories, and responds with warmth, patience, and genuine personalization.

📋
3
Track and improve

Sessions are saved automatically. Well-being trends are tracked over time. Families and caregivers receive summaries, giving everyone a clearer picture of how their loved one is doing.
