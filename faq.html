Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about accounts, connections, the companion, check-ins, privacy, and what Techable does—and does not—do automatically.

Choose a question below. If something is not covered, email hello@techable.org. For the formal description of data handling, read the Data & Privacy Policy.

Accounts and sign-in

Which account type should I use?

Use Senior for your own companion and profile, Family & Friends to connect with a senior, Community Center Manager to manage a centre and its residents, or Service Provider for assigned care work. If you sign in through the wrong doorway, Techable reads your saved role and routes you to the correct workspace.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Open the sign-in page for any role, enter your email, and select Forgot your password?. Firebase sends a password reset email. Check spam or junk mail if it does not appear, and confirm that you entered the address used for your account.

Why does my manager or provider account say pending?

Manager and provider verification is reviewed by Techable. You can sign in while a review is pending, but protected actions such as provider assignment or publishing announcements remain unavailable until approval. If a submission is rejected, the page shows the review note and allows a replacement submission.

Family and provider access

How does a family member connect to a senior?

The family member enters the senior’s email address. Techable looks it up on its own server, a few tries at a time, and finds only a senior who has turned on Let my family find me on their Family screen (a new senior is asked in the first-visit welcome). The request stays pending until the senior says yes on the Family screen of their Techable app, or, for a centre-managed senior, until either the senior or their manager approves it from the Members tab. A pending request cannot read the senior’s profile, sessions or life story. A standalone senior, with no centre manager, is always asked themselves. The senior sees everyone connected to them under Connected to you, can remove anyone, and can block a request from someone they do not know.

Who can approve a family connection?

Only the manager recorded on that resident can approve it. Another manager cannot read or decide the request. A rejected family member may submit a fresh request later.

How does a service provider receive access?

An admin-approved manager chooses a verified provider and assigns a resident. The provider can then view that assigned resident’s profile and sessions and add care notes. Assignment to one resident does not grant access to any other resident.

Messages, stories and centres

How can I send a voice note or photo to my senior?

On the Family Dashboard, use the card headed Send … a message, a voice note or a photo and choose a linked senior. Write a message, record a voice note of up to 90 seconds, or add a photo. You can preview recordings and photos before selecting Send. A voice note and a photo sent together appear as two messages, with your written message on the first.

Messages and their recordings or photos are stored in Firebase (Cloud Firestore), readable only by the sender and the senior. The first time someone sends the senior a message, the senior is asked whether they know them: nothing that person sent is announced, played or shown until the senior says yes, and the senior can block them instead. The companion shows or plays the message in the senior's chat and marks it opened. When the senior opens a message in the chat, its words become part of that conversation, which the AI and voice services process like anything else said in the chat. Either side can delete it. Nothing is sent by email, text message or push notification.

Why can't my senior receive a voice note or photo?

They need their own login to Techable to receive these messages in their chat. The first time you send one, they are asked whether they know you, and it is shown only once they say yes. A senior whose profile was created by a centre may not have a login of their own. Their name is shown in the recipient list but cannot be chosen — or, if none of your linked seniors has a login, the card says so instead of showing the form. To write to their centre team, use the Message their centre team box on the Family Dashboard.

If your browser cannot record audio or microphone permission is refused, you can still send text and photos.

What is the life story book, and who can read it?

Stories a senior tells the companion or adds themselves are kept in chapters on their profile, under My Story. A long memory told in an ordinary chat can be offered for the book: it waits on the senior's own device and goes into the book only if they choose to keep it. Memories that mention health, money, violence or a secret are never offered. The senior, their connected centre manager, linked family and assigned service providers can read the book, using the same access they already have to the profile. Linked family can open each senior's life story from the Family Dashboard. The senior can edit or delete any story, and print it or save it as a PDF on their own device.

How do I ask to join a centre?

As a senior, answer the centre question on your first visit, or tap the button on the centre card at the top of the chat screen. Choose a public community centre and tap Ask to join; only centres Techable has verified are listed. The centre's manager sees your request, including your name and the centre, and decides whether to approve or decline it. Once approved, the centre can see and change your profile, memories, life story and form details, see your chat summaries and daily check-ins, and its companion settings apply, like for any member. A centre can remove you, but cannot delete your own record. Leaving a centre is not possible in the app yet: contact Techable to be removed, or delete the account.

How does a manager approve a senior's join request?

In the manager's Members tab, open Seniors asking to join. Check the senior's name and requested centre, then choose Approve or Decline. Approving connects their own account to your centre: you can see and change their profile, memories, life story and form details, and see their chat summaries and check-ins, like any member. Only a manager Techable has verified can receive requests. You can remove a senior who joined this way later; you cannot delete their record, which stays theirs.

Companion, voice, and check-ins

What happens when a senior first signs in?

On first sign-in the senior is asked what to call them, their language, interests and family. Their answers are saved to their profile and memories so the companion can be more personal.

Do I need to save my chats?

No. Chats save themselves: there is no Save button. A chat is saved when the senior finishes it, leaves the page, or pauses for a long time. The full transcript stays in the browser. Firestore keeps a summary of each chat — how long it was, how many messages, its topics — and any memories the companion picks up from what the senior says. A Guess My Day check-in also keeps its record for the authorized dashboards: the reported mood, the reason and the senior's own words about their day, the companion's reply, anything flagged, and the follow-up.

What do the AI and voice settings control?

A manager can enable or disable AI replies and spoken output for managed resident companions. Techable stores only those two companion settings in a separate Firestore document. Turning AI off uses the local fallback replies; turning voice off stops automatic spoken output on the resident companion.

What is “Guess My Day”?

It is a short daily check-in about mood, a possible reason, and one follow-up. A completed check-in is saved with the senior’s sessions. Authorized centre managers, connected family members, and assigned providers may see the relevant wellbeing information according to their access.

Does an urgent check-in call or message somebody?

No. Techable records and flags urgent wording so an authorized person can see it in their dashboard, but it does not automatically notify, contact, or alert a manager, family member, provider, clinician, or emergency service. Techable is not an emergency service. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services directly.

Does the companion diagnose medical conditions?

No. The companion and wellbeing forms support conversation and human review; they do not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional medical care. Health forms must be reviewed with the senior before they are saved.

Privacy, storage, and deletion

Who can see a senior’s information?

The senior and their routed centre manager can access the resident record. A family member needs an active connection, and a provider needs an explicit assignment. Firestore security rules enforce these relationships; a link that is still pending grants no resident access.

Which external services process information?

Firebase Authentication and Firestore provide accounts and application storage. OpenAI may process companion and sentence-understanding requests and text for speech; Google Cloud TTS may process text for speech when configured. OpenWeather processes a location when weather context is requested. Supabase stores manager and provider verification documents through private, time-limited upload links. Vercel hosts the website and serverless API routes.

How do I delete my account and data?

Seniors can use Delete Account Permanently from their account flow, and managers can use it in Profile settings. Family members and providers should contact Techable for assistance. Browser deletion removes the account and the records it owns or manages before deleting sign-in access.

Some incoming records created and controlled by another account may remain—for example, an assignment created by a manager. Uploaded verification documents are also not deleted automatically. Contact hello@techable.org for a complete review and removal request.

How long does Techable retain data?

Techable does not currently apply an automatic retention or expiry schedule. Records remain until an authorized user deletes them or Techable completes a supported removal request. This retention position should be reviewed before each production launch and for the laws of the operating country.

Should I enter government or insurance identification numbers?

Do not put Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid, or insurance identification numbers into the companion’s “My details for forms” card. Enter those only on the official form that requires them. A centre’s separate registration form may request an identification field according to its own process.

Still need help?

Contact hello@techable.org. Do not email passwords, identity documents, medical identification numbers, or other highly sensitive records.
