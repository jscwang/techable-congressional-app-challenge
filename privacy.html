Data & Privacy Policy

How Techable collects, uses, protects, and shares information across senior registration, family access, community management, and provider tools.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Overview

Techable supports older adults, family members, community center managers, and service providers. This notice describes the information used to create accounts, manage center registrations, connect authorized people, provide companion and care-related tools, and operate the service.

This policy is a product privacy notice for Techable. It is not a substitute for formal legal advice, and it should be reviewed before production launch in each operating country.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, role, authentication provider, and profile photo if supplied by Google sign-in.
  • Senior registration details such as age, contact details, nationality, optional date of birth, optional identification information, address, and emergency contact information.
  • Community center details such as center name, address, manager contact information, member records, registrations, attendance, and center profile settings.
  • Family and service provider details such as relationship, organization, provider type, linked residents, assigned residents, messages, voice recordings and photos sent to a senior, notes, and activity needed to provide the service.
  • Resident support data such as profile information, memories, well-being forms, benefit-application drafts, conversation records, daily check-ins, escalation flags, and care session information added by authorized users.
  • Manager and provider verification documents submitted for account review.
  • Technical information needed to run the service, including timestamps, authentication IDs, database record IDs, and basic error or security information.

How We Use Data

  • To create and secure user accounts.
  • To submit and review senior registrations with selected community centers.
  • To ask the senior before any family connection is made. A connection request stays pending until it is approved by the senior in their Techable app, or, for a center-managed resident, by the senior or their center manager. The senior sees every connected family account, can remove any of them, and can block a request from someone they do not know. A senior can be found by their email address only while they have “Let my family find me” turned on; the lookup runs on Techable's server, a few tries at a time per family account.
  • To let an approved manager assign a verified service provider to a specific resident.
  • To provide resident profiles, memory support, care notes, well-being views, dashboards, and community center tools.
  • To prevent misuse, debug problems, improve reliability, and maintain the service.

Role-Based Sharing

Family access to a resident must be approved by the resident, or for a center-managed resident by their center manager; a pending request grants no resident access. The resident can remove a connection at any time.

Senior registration data is shared with the chosen community center manager so they can review and manage the registration. Resident data may be visible to the resident, their routed center manager, family members with an active connection, and service providers explicitly assigned to that resident. A pending family request grants no resident access. An assignment or connection to one resident does not grant access to another.

Techable does not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service Providers And International Processing

  • Firebase Authentication and Firestore provide sign-in and application data storage.
  • OpenAI may process companion prompts and replies, sentence-understanding requests, and speech requests. Google Cloud TTS may process text for spoken output when configured.
  • OpenWeather processes a location when the app requests current weather context.
  • Supabase stores manager and provider verification documents in a private bucket reached through time-limited upload and review links.
  • Vercel hosts the website and serverless API routes and may process ordinary request information needed to serve them.

These providers may process information in countries outside the user's location under their own contractual and legal arrangements.

Storage And Security

Firestore security rules restrict application records by ownership, role, active family connection, manager route, or provider assignment. Verification documents are kept private and accessed through short-lived signed links. Manager companion settings are stored separately from the manager's account profile and contain only AI-enabled and voice-enabled values. We use reasonable technical controls, but no online system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

AI And Memories

Techable may send resident memories, profile details, conversation context, check-in wording, and a weather summary to configured AI services to create a more personal companion experience. Users should avoid adding information they are not authorized to share. Sensitive care or medical information should only be added where appropriate and with permission.

Techable is not a diagnostic or emergency service. Urgent wording may be saved and flagged in authorized dashboards, but the app does not automatically notify, contact, or alert a manager, family member, provider, clinician, or emergency service.

Family Messages To Seniors

A linked family member can send a written message, a voice recording of up to 90 seconds, or a photo to a senior who has their own Techable login. Messages and their recordings or photos are stored in Firebase (Cloud Firestore), readable only by the sender and the senior. The first time someone sends the senior a message, the senior is asked whether they know them: nothing that person sent is announced, played or shown until the senior says yes, and the senior can block them instead. The companion shows or plays the message in the senior's chat and marks it opened. When the senior opens a message in the chat, its words become part of that conversation, which the AI and voice services process like anything else said in the chat. Either side can delete it. Nothing is sent by email, text message or push notification.

Life Story Book

Stories a senior tells the companion or adds themselves are kept in chapters on their profile. A long memory told in an ordinary chat can be offered for the book: it waits on the senior's own device and goes into the book only if they choose to keep it. Memories that mention health, money, violence or a secret are never offered. The senior, their connected centre manager, linked family and assigned service providers can read the book, using the same access they already have to the profile. The senior can edit or delete any story, and print it or save it as a PDF on their own device.

Joining A Centre

A senior can ask a public community centre to connect their account. The centre's manager sees the request, including the senior's name and the centre, and decides whether to approve or decline it. Only a centre whose manager Techable has verified can be asked. Once approved, the centre can see and change the senior's profile, memories, life story and form details, see their chat summaries and daily check-ins, and its companion settings apply, like for any member. A centre can remove a senior who joined this way, but cannot delete the senior's own record. Leaving a centre is not possible in the app yet: contact Techable at the address below to be removed, or delete the account.

Getting Started And Saving Chats

On first sign-in the senior is asked what to call them, their language, interests and family. Their answers are saved to their profile and memories. They can also choose a community centre and ask to join it, as described above. Every question can be skipped.

Chats save themselves: there is no Save button. A chat is saved when the senior finishes it, leaves the page, or pauses for a long time. The full transcript stays in the browser. Firestore keeps a summary of each chat — how long it was, how many messages, its topics — and any memories the companion picks up from what the senior says, saved to their profile. A Guess My Day check-in also keeps its record: the reported mood, the reason and the senior's own words about their day, the companion's reply, any concerns or urgent wording flagged, and the follow-up.

Retention

Techable does not currently apply an automatic retention or expiry schedule. Records remain until an authorized user deletes them or Techable completes a supported removal request. Retention requirements should be reviewed before production launch in each operating country.

For center-uploaded forms, a verified center manager sends a blank PDF to OpenAI to extract questions, reviews the digital copy, and selects its recipients. Techable does not retain the original PDF. A senior may fill matching fields from their saved details or dictate answers. Answers are saved for the sending center to read only after the senior explicitly reviews and confirms saving; this does not submit an official application. Saved form copies and answers are readable only by the sending account and selected senior, and are included in their account-deletion cleanup.

Family Chat supports private two-way text messages between a senior and an approved family connection, after the senior permits messages. Dictation sends a recording to OpenAI to fill a draft for review before sending. Chat text is not automatically added to AI memories. Blocking or removing the connection stops new messages; previously delivered history remains private to its original two participants and is included in account-deletion cleanup. Center managers and service providers cannot read this chat.

Your Choices

  • You may choose not to provide optional fields such as date of birth, ID number, address, or organization unless your center requires them separately.
  • You may ask the relevant community center manager to update, unlink, or remove resident and registration records they control.
  • Seniors and managers have a self-service Delete Account Permanently flow. Family members and providers may request deletion or correction by contacting Techable.

Deletion Limitations

The self-service flow attempts to remove the account's owned and managed Firestore records before deleting sign-in access. Some incoming records created and controlled by another account may remain, such as a provider assignment created by a manager. Verification documents stored in Supabase are not currently removed automatically by browser account deletion. Contact Techable for a complete review and removal request. If a deletion step fails, the sign-in is kept so the account holder can retry.
