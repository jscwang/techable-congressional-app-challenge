⚠️ Danger Zone

This cannot be undone. It deletes your sign-in and profile, your centres and the registrations sent to them, the residents you created with their saved conversations and check-ins, and attendance records. Seniors who joined your centre with their own login keep their records; they are only taken off your members list. If anything cannot be removed, your account is kept so you can try again.
